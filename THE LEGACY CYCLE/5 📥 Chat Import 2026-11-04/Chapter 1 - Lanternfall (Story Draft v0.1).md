# Chapter 1 — Lanternfall (Story Draft v0.1)

Part of [[Chat Import Hub - Roblox Action MMO]]. Uses the Shattered Sky setting from [[Game Plan v0.1 - Action MMO]]. The existing Legacy Cycle opening is in [[Prologue Script]] and [[Act 1 Quest Outline]]; compare before choosing.

## At a glance

A masked stranger steals the flame that keeps the fog away from Lanternfall. The player, who can remember what the fog makes everyone else forget, climbs the lighthouse to bring it back. They free a guardian the whole island forgot and give an old woman back a name she lost.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Island | **Lanternfall**: a fishing village and lighthouse at the edge of the fog |
| Play length | About 45 minutes for the main story, plus 10–15 minutes of optional side quests |
| The fog | **The Hush**: whatever falls into it is forgotten by everyone, even the people who loved it |
| Player | **The Wakeling**: wakes with no memory and is the only one who can remember what the Hush takes |
| Mini-boss | **The Saltknight**: a suit of armor nobody remembers |
| Boss | **The Hollow Keeper**: the lighthouse's forgotten guardian, taken over by the Hush |
| New Echoes | 6: 3 starters, 2 earned from bosses, 1 hidden |
| Unlocks at the end | Arena, raid, and a glimpse of Island 2 |

**The feeling we want:** "I want to find out who I am," plus "I helped someone remember." Dark themes (forgetting, loss) with a hopeful ending and no gore. Defeated enemies break into light or fog.

## Cast

Seven characters carry Chapter 1. The emotional center is a girl whose grandmother is forgetting her.

| Character | Who they are | What they want | Their role in the chapter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Wakeling** (player) | Washed up on a drifting rock with no memory. An Echo chose them. | To find out who they are | Hero. Uses the player's Roblox display name. |
| **Tamsin** | A 12-year-old lantern apprentice. Carries a notebook of things people have forgotten. | To keep her grandmother from forgetting her | The person to care about. Gives the side quests. |
| **Old Wren** | The lantern keeper, Tamsin's grandmother. Kind, sharp, but the rising Hush is taking her memories. | To keep the light burning one more year | The heart of the chapter. Her lost memory is the payoff. |
| **Captain Hale** | Leader of the Skywardens, the island's small guard | To protect the village with too few people | Gives the main quest. Opens the arena later. |
| **Juno** | A cocky Skywarden cadet who wants to be the best fighter on the island | To prove herself | A friendly rival. Her duel teaches the parry and leads into the arena. |
| **The Masked Figure** | A silent stranger in a white mask who appears at Echo shrines | Unknown (to us: collecting every island's flame) | Steals the lantern's flame. The saga's central mystery. |
| **Rowan** (forgotten) | Wren's husband. He fell into the Hush 40 years ago and became the Hollow Keeper. | To keep his promise to guard the light | The boss, and the forgotten person the player gives back to Wren. |

## Story beats

The chapter runs in 10 beats, about 45 minutes in total. No scene runs over 60 seconds, and each beat teaches one new thing.

1. **Cold open — "What is your name?" (1 min, scene).** A rock drifts through grey fog. Whispers. A small light chooses you. A voice asks your name, and you can't remember it. *Hook: who am I?*
2. **The drifting rock (5 min).** Walk, attack, dash. Fight Hushlings, small fog creatures that burst into mist. At a broken shrine, pick 1 of 3 starter Echoes. The rock crashes onto Lanternfall's shore. *Teaches: movement, M1 combo, dash, first Echo move.*
3. **Lanternfall village (5 min).** Tamsin finds you. Wren welcomes you warmly, then forgets Tamsin's name mid-sentence: we show the Hush, we don't explain it. Captain Hale asks for help because the fog is rising. *Teaches: the hub, the quest marker, talking to NPCs.*
4. **The docks (8 min).** The fog rolls in and Hushlings attack. Defend the boats. Juno challenges you to a duel and loses (or wins). Afterwards she grins: "Again. Tomorrow." *Teaches: block, parry, and the idea of the arena.*
5. **Tamsin's notebook (optional, 10–15 min).** Find 5 forgotten objects around the island: a ring, a fishing hook, a carved bird, a song sheet and a coat. Each has a one-line memory. *Reward: the hidden Echo. Secret: all 5 belonged to Rowan.*
6. **The Salt Caves (8 min).** Follow the old path to the lighthouse. Mini-boss: the Saltknight. Defeated, he breaks into light and leaves his Echo. His memory shows him guarding a path for someone with a lantern. *Teaches: a short mini-boss fight with clear telegraphs.*
7. **The lantern goes dark (45 s, scene).** At the top of the lighthouse, the Masked Figure lifts the flame out of the lantern and vanishes. The Hush floods the village. Wren looks at Tamsin and asks, "Who are you, dear?" *The personal stakes.*
8. **The climb (8 min).** A vertical level up the outside of the lighthouse through the storm. Gaps that need your Echo move to cross. Enemy waves. *Teaches: using Echo moves to travel, not just to fight.*
9. **Boss — the Hollow Keeper (3–4 min, 3 phases).** A tall figure of fog and old iron with a dead lantern for a head. Phase 3: he hesitates when your Echo glows, as if he recognizes it. Defeated, he becomes light, and his Echo, Keeper's Light, bonds with you. Memory: a young Wren and Rowan lighting the lantern together.
10. **Payoff and cliffhanger (2 min, two short scenes).** You relight the lantern with Keeper's Light. You tell Wren about Rowan. For one moment she remembers him, and Tamsin's name comes back too. Tamsin writes "Rowan" in her notebook: "Now someone will always remember." Then, on the cliff, the Masked Figure removes a glove. Their hand has the same Echo mark as yours. The lantern beam sweeps the fog, and a second island rises out of it.

**After the chapter:** Hale opens the Skywarden arena, Juno is waiting there, and the raid unlocks. The raid is a 3–4 player trip into the Hushdeep under the lighthouse, where the Saltknight was guarding something.

## Key scenes

These are first-draft scripts for the four scenes that carry the story. Each line is kept short so it fits a phone screen and can be translated later.

### Scene 1 — Cold open

*Grey fog. A rock drifts. Faint whispers, too many to understand. A small light flickers awake beside the player.*

> **VOICE (soft, from the light):** You're still here. Everyone else forgot.
>
> **VOICE:** What's your name?
>
> *The player's name appears, letter by letter, then breaks apart into fog.*
>
> **VOICE:** ...Then let's find it. Together.

### Scene 7 — The lantern goes dark

*Top of the lighthouse. The great lantern burns. The Masked Figure stands beside it, calm. They reach into the flame and lift it out like a bird. The light dies. Below, the fog pours over the cliffs.*

> **TAMSIN (running in):** Grandma! The light—
>
> **WREN (gentle, confused):** Oh. Hello, dear. Who are you?
>
> *Tamsin freezes. Her notebook falls open to a page that just says "TAMSIN" in Wren's handwriting.*

### Scene 9 — After the Hollow Keeper

*The Keeper kneels. Fog peels away, and inside is a lantern-keeper's coat, the same one from Tamsin's notebook quest.*

> **HOLLOW KEEPER (a quiet man's voice now):** I promised I'd keep it lit.
>
> **HOLLOW KEEPER:** Tell her... I kept my promise.
>
> *He breaks into warm light. The light circles the player and settles: the Keeper's Light Echo. Memory scene (15 s): a young Wren laughing, a young Rowan lifting her up to light the lantern for the first time.*

### Scene 10 — Payoff and cliffhanger

> **PLAYER (choice):** "His name was Rowan." / "He kept his promise."
>
> **WREN (a long pause):** ...Rowan. Rowan. *(She turns.)* Tamsin, love, put the kettle on.
>
> **TAMSIN (crying, smiling, writing):** Now someone will always remember.

*Cut to the cliff. The Masked Figure watches the relit lantern and pulls off one glove. The Echo mark on their hand is the same as the player's.*

> **MASKED FIGURE:** You remember. You shouldn't be able to.
>
> **MASKED FIGURE:** Unless he kept his promise too.

*The lantern beam sweeps the fog. Far away, a second island rises. Title card: CHAPTER 1 — LANTERNFALL. CONTINUED IN CHAPTER 2.*

## Echoes introduced in Chapter 1

Chapter 1 brings in all 6 launch Echoes. The player picks 1 starter. The others are earned through bosses, the side quests and repeat play, never bought.

| Echo | Looks like | Role | How you get it | Memory it holds (one line) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Kindle** | A small fox made of embers | Damage | Starter choice | A hearth fire in a house nobody remembers |
| **Tidewarden** | A turtle with a shell of sea-glass | Tank | Starter choice | A harbor wall that held back a storm |
| **Wisp-Lark** | A bird of wind and paper | Control and speed | Starter choice | A kite string slipping through a child's fingers |
| **Saltknight** | Empty armor crusted with salt | Tank and damage | Mini-boss drop | Guarding a path for someone with a lantern |
| **Keeper's Light** | A lantern flame with soft wings | Support (heals, light) | Boss reward, guaranteed | Rowan and Wren lighting the lantern for the first time |
| **Moth of Small Things** (hidden) | A pale moth with ink-mark wings | Control | Find all 5 forgotten objects | All 5 objects belonged to the same forgotten man |

**Rule for every Echo:** 4 moves plus 1 ultimate, one clear role, and a one-line memory. Mastery unlocks the full memory scene, then the awakened form. The two starters you didn't pick can be earned later from shrine quests, so no choice locks you out.

## Mysteries planted

Chapter 1 answers one question (who the Hollow Keeper was) and asks four new ones. The true answers go in the private story bible, not in this note.

| Mystery | Where it's planted | Which layer of player sees it | Planned payoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Why can the Wakeling remember what the Hush erases? | Scene 1, the voice | Everyone | Saga finale |
| Who is the Masked Figure, and why do they have the same Echo mark? | Scene 10, the glove | Everyone | Season 1 finale (Chapter 3) |
| Who is "he" in "unless he kept his promise too"? | Scene 10 | Curious players | Chapter 3 |
| What was the Saltknight guarding in the Hushdeep? | Beat 6 memory, the raid | Curious players, raid players | Chapter 2 or the raid's final boss |
| Why did Rowan's 5 objects survive when he was forgotten? | Hidden Echo quest | Obsessed players (theory videos) | Chapter 4 |

**Already answered in Chapter 1:** the Hollow Keeper was Rowan, Wren's husband, still keeping his promise to guard the light.
