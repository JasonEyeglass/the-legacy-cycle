# Research: what made Roblox hits and MMOs work

Research from the 2026-11-04 chat. Part of [[Chat Import Hub - Roblox Action MMO]]. Related: [[Competitor Baseline - Rogue Lineage and Deepwoken]].

## 1. How the big Roblox games got famous

### Blox Fruits
- **Not an overnight success.** The same developers had made earlier games: Elemental Battlegrounds (PvP magic, 410M+ visits) and Parkour Simulator (died soon after launch).
- Launched mid-2019 as "Blox Piece" and stayed under 20K concurrent players for its first year. Growth began in late 2020, and the big breakthrough came in 2022.
- In December 2019, copyright pressure from Toei Animation forced a rebrand to "Blox Fruits"; islands, characters and abilities were renamed. **This is why an original world is safer.**
- Crossed 60 billion total visits in March 2026.
- What worked:
    - **Simple loop:** explore → fight harder enemies → farm loot → get stronger.
    - **Real choices:** one fruit active at a time, each with tradeoffs.
    - **Return hooks:** a 2-hour cooldown on free fruit rolls; trading that drives server hopping.
    - **Creators:** YouTube sneak peeks, about 1.5M Discord members, fan ideas added to the game.
    - **Monetization:** permanent fruits and EXP boosts (speed-ups), designed so paying players have no huge edge.

### Jujutsu Shenanigans and the "battlegrounds" genre
- Launched Apr 17, 2022; 20-player servers; about 6.2B visits; all-time peak of about 697K concurrent players (2026-10-23).
- Most anime battlegrounds games follow the template set by The Strongest Battlegrounds (2023): a small arena, characters with 4–6 moves, no level gap between new and veteran players, cinematic ultimates, cosmetic-only sales.
- Why it works on Roblox:
    - **Clip-friendly ultimates:** free marketing on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
    - **4–6 abilities:** fits phone touch controls (most players are on mobile).
    - **Short sessions:** someone with 200 hours in Blox Fruits can drop in for a 20-minute fight.
- Risk: most of these games use anime IP without a license and run on informal publisher tolerance.

### Genre trend
- Fighting games are the fastest-growing genre on Roblox in 2026. Players expect responsive combat, satisfying animations, balance and progression.

### Deepwoken
- Proof that a story-heavy, hardcore RPG works on Roblox: environmental storytelling, factions, deep lore. Won "Best New Experience" at the Innovation Awards '22. It costs 400 Robux to enter and has permadeath. See [[Competitor Baseline - Rogue Lineage and Deepwoken]].

## 2. How WoW and FFXIV build their stories
- **FFXIV (Shadowbringers):**
    - **A personal connection.** The side character Tesleen's sacrifice bonded players to the world; show, don't tell.
    - **One clear plot at a time.** Earlier expansions lost focus.
    - **Payoff from earlier lore,** planned years ahead.
    - **Hope under the darkness.** Dark plots, optimistic tone.
- **WoW:** much of the story is told through raids and dungeons, the group content players repeat. Better suited to a hack-and-slash game than long cutscenes.

## 3. How they keep players (and how to do it for free)
- **WoW:** daily quests (from The Burning Crusade on), daily dungeons and raids (Wrath of the Lich King), several paths to top gear, and a steady update cadence (Dragonflight promised 6 updates a year and delivered them on time). Over-grindy reputation systems made players resentful.
- **FFXIV:** Yoshida's strategy is to keep attracting new players rather than cling to each one. He encourages breaks between patches to prevent burnout, and communicates plans openly (82+ "Letter from the Producer" streams).
- **For a free Roblox game:** short daily and weekly goals (WoW), easy to come back after a break (FFXIV), and a public roadmap with regular dev streams.

## 4. Roblox money basics
- Roblox paid creators $1.5B in 2025, up 63%.
- The DevEx cash-out rate is $0.0038 per Robux, and $0.0054 for spending by US players 18+ (since June 8, 2026).
- Cosmetics-only is fairer but earns less per player, so the cosmetics must look great: weapon skins, ultimate effects, kill effects, auras, emotes, titles.

## 5. Other research from the chat
- **Live-service story cadence:** Genshin Impact ships a new version about every 6 weeks with new main-story acts. Destiny 2 planted the "15th Wish" in 2018 and paid it off years later. Its team aimed for 6–8 weeks of self-contained story per season feeding a bigger arc. Players later called the template predictable and disliked the weekly drip feed, so Bungie switched to episodes.
- **Platforms:** about 80% of Roblox users are on mobile, 17% on desktop and 3% on console.
