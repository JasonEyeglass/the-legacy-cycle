## 🧠 Decisions
- **No heirs** (logged in [[Decisions|Decisions Log]]): one character per player. Retirement, heirs, heirlooms, bloodline traits and reputation carry-over are cut as too complicated for kids. "Legacy" now means past heroes, shown through playable boss and Echo memories. The 3 seals come from region bosses; the final choice (let go, take control, join the Void) carries the lesson.
