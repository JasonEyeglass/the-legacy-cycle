Back to [[The Legacy Cycle]]. The calls made today are in [[Decisions]].

## Verified in playtests

- The whole loop works: menu, Play, the loading pan, Continue, the game, M, Back to Main Menu, Play, and back in the game. The HUD and movement come back.
- A server settings save cleans junk values and applies the volumes, shadows and post effects.
- The inventory page clears the ground by 0.94 studs. In first person, the menu sits centred and fits the screen.
- The console was clean.
