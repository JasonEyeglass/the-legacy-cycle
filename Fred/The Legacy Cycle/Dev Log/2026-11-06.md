Back to [[The Legacy Cycle]]. The calls made today are in [[Decisions]].

- **Loading screen:** a cinematic pan over Havenbrook (town, keep, ruins and market), a loading bar, and Forge your hero or Continue. See [[Join Flow]].
- **Claude Design mockups** of the loading screen and the creator: https://claude.ai/artifact/3uDM3AaNB91bq9dboo7nb8

- **Main menu** before the loading screen: Play, Character Customization and Settings. The username was removed at the user's request.
- **Settings:** Music and Sound effects sliders, plus Camera shake and Low specs toggles, saved per player. I added the SoundService Music and Effects groups. See [[Settings]].
- **Inventory:** the slots are now Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Gloves, Weapon and Boots. Armor was renamed Chestplate, and Leggings was added for the lower body.
- **Floating menu:**
  - A floor check stops tall pages clipping the ground.
  - First-person placement.
  - An Embers (skill tree) placeholder page.
  - Back to Main Menu, with a camera-rise return animation.
  - A flickering firelight, ember red on Embers.
  - Other players see a ghost of your open menu: its shape, title and light, not its contents.
  - See [[Floating Menu]].
- **Shrine of Embers:** the new brazier mesh replaces the old blocky shrine in Havenbrook, and its prompts still work. It has two fire versions to choose between, native (A) and a Blender Mantaflow flipbook (B), plus a flicker, breath, near-flare and use-burst script. See [[Shrine VFX]].
  - The user picked the native version and toned the fire down to an ember smoulder.
- **Absorbing the Ember:** a once-per-player, saved B prompt at the shrine. The spark rises, hangs and arcs into the chest, and the shrine goes dark and cold for that player. A faint Ember arc appears in the HUD and the Embers page lights. See [[Shrine VFX]].
- **Floating menu light:** a cool blue-white light (range 20, shadows) and a cool world tint while open. See [[Floating Menu]].
- **Floating menu redesign:** the user called the old menu slop. I made two directions on a Claude Design canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/4wrPSPaMRBqVdkPurpwu4T) and the user picked **B · Grimoire**. It is rebuilt in Studio as a 760×440 open book with chapters I–IV and an Overview ledger. All four chapters, chapter switching, the hero preview and the `MenuPage` relay were checked, with a clean console. See [[Floating Menu]].
- **Floating menu outline:** a glowing light outline round the panel, in the light's colour, with a highlight circling the rim. It is checked on the front, the back and after the Inventory morph.
- **Floating menu button states:** hover, pressed, gamepad-selected and disabled looks, a hand cursor and a gold gamepad ring.
- **Floating menu, direction D "Codex":** the user asked to combine the grimoire (B) with a modern tabbed inventory (C, from a reference image).
  - It is rebuilt with tabs across the top, picker cards on the left page, and a detail page with one gold action on the right.
  - The light, the outline and the world tint changed from blue-white to warm candle gold, at the user's request.
  - All four chapters were checked in a playtest: the Pack view, an empty slot, spending a point (3 left, Strength 3) and the warm light in the world. The console was clean. See [[Floating Menu]].

  - The book is painted, with ribbon chapter bookmarks, a wax-seal close, slot plates round an ink knight, drop-cap titles, ink drawings and a ribbon-seal action.
  - Checked in-world: Overview (with the sepia hero portrait), Inventory (with the katana drawing) and Stats. The console was clean.
- **Floating menu outline removed**, at the user's request, along with its 18 px margin. The drop cap is now cropped (`dropcap_left`) so the title follows the letter closely.
- **HUD and main menu redrawn with painted art.** The user generated three more sheets from my prompts (HUD, main menu, ink icons). I cut them into 82 pieces on three atlases (upload approved) and added them to `GrimoireArt`, with three helpers:
  - `Art.crop` fills a bar without squashing its art.
  - `Art.slice` nine-slices a piece.
  - `Art.states` swaps a button between its rest, hover, pressed and disabled art.
- **Vitals:** the curved arcs are kept, at the user's request.
  - Each fill segment shows its own slice of the painted HP, Stamina or Ember strip.
  - The rim is gilt, with gold star ends, and the heart and wing-foot glyphs sit on parchment badges.
  - The user saw jagged rims. Rotated frames aren't antialiased, so the rim and groove are now smooth `UIStroke` circles clipped to the arc.
- **Progress:** a gilt diamond badge, a "Level N" ribbon and a gilt-framed XP bar with the painted fill.
- **Minimap:** a parchment disc and a gilt compass ring that turns with your heading. It has a wax-seal N, a cream arrow for you, a gold quest star, red enemy dots and a ribbon for the zone name.
- **Main menu:**
