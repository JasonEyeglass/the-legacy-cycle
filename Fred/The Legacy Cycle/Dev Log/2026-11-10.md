# 2026-11-10

Back to [[The Legacy Cycle]]. The previous day's work is in [[2026-11-09]].

## Built

- **Running lunge (new animation, not wired in yet):** a one-handed katana thrust out of the Katana Run, made on the user's R15 `Workspace.Rig`.
  - **Saved as** `Workspace.Rig.AnimSaves.RunningLunge`: 27 frames at 30 fps (0.87 s), Action priority, not looped, with a `Hit` marker on frame 11 (0.33 s).
  - **Five beats:**
    - Idle (f1): the run's mid-stride, the same pose RunningSlash starts from.
    - Anticipation (f5): plant the left foot, coil right, hand chambered by the ribs with the blade already pointing ahead.
    - Action (f8 push-off, f11 contact): a deep lunge onto the right foot, arm straight, blade level at chest height about 9.5 studs out, left arm thrown back.
    - Follow-through (f15): the slide carries on and the tip dips.
    - Recovery (f21 to f27): back to the run pose.
  - **The timing fits `Config.RunningHeavy` as it is:** HitDelay 0.33, length 0.87 s like RunningSlash, and the lunge push lands during the thrust.
  - **Source:** `ServerStorage.AnimTraining.RunningLunge`. `bake(rig)` rebuilds it; edit `KEYS` to tweak a beat. Preview statues of f1, 5, 8, 11, 15 and 21 are in `ServerStorage.AnimTraining.RunningLungePreview`.
  - **Edit checks:**
    - No joint gaps.
    - The worst box overlap is 0.41, at the push-off. The game's Katana Run reaches 0.64.
    - Soles stay on the ground.
    - The baked poses match the solver exactly.
    - It wasn't playtested.
  - **Not done:**
    - It needs uploading for an id.
    - Using it means pointing `RunningHeavy.Anim` at it (or adding it as a second running attack) and measuring a new `SlashFx.Cuts` entry. A thrust probably wants a stab streak more than a crescent.

- **Open world: the continent and the new Havenbrook (/build-world, off the slice path except Havenbrook itself).** Built in Studio Edit mode; not playtested.
  - **Where:** a whole continent, 10,240 studs square, centred at (16000, 0, 0), about 11k studs from the dev area so neither sees the other. The dev spawn is unchanged and the old town in `Workspace.World` is untouched, quest objects included.
  - **Terrain:** `ServerStorage.WorldGen` writes it from seeded noise in 82 s (`WG.clear(); WG.build()`).
    - Regions: Havenbrook meadows (south), Ethereal Forest (west), Frostveil Mountains (north), Ashen Wastes (east, terraced mesas), Shadowlands (north-east, spires), and the Forgotten Realm island (north-west, cut off by a sea channel).
    - Five roads from the town gates, the meandering River Haven past the town's east side, three lakes (Mirrormere, Stillwater, Frost Tarn), sand only on the coast.
  - **Kit:** `Documents/Lihfolk_Vault/Legacy Cycle World/build_havenbrook_kit.py` builds `out/Havenbrook.glb` (25 pieces, 10,200 tris) and `out/Nature.glb` (8 pieces, 6,216 tris). `check_mesh` passes both.
  - **Placer:** `ServerStorage.WorldPlacer.place()` rebuilds `Workspace.Continent` in about 4 s:
    - Havenbrook: a 220-stud wall ring with four gatehouses and eight towers, cobbled cross streets, the paved square with fountain and four stalls, Orla's guild hall on the square's north side, the inn on its east side, Tobin's smithy by the north gate, 89 houses in street and back rows, lamps and props.
    - Outskirts: the Old Gate crack outside the north gate (terrain pit carved), the collapsed watchtower, the windmill, and a ploughed, fenced farm plot with farmhouse and barn.
    - Bridges where the East Road and Shadow Road cross the river.
    - Nature: 3,265 trees and rocks scattered per region (2,352 in the forest).
    - Everything is blockout boxes until the GLBs are imported into `ServerStorage.WorldKit`.
  - **Story anchors:** `Continent.Havenbrook.Story` and `Continent.Outskirts.Story` hold invisible `Anchor_*` parts (EmberLantern, QuestBoard, Orla, Tobin, TownSpawn, TownsfolkStop1-6, OldGateEntrance, Bryn) for when the quest objects move over.
  - **Dev travel:** `ServerScriptService.DevWorldTravel` (Studio only) puts a pad beside the dev spawn: hold G to go to the new square, and G at the square to come back.
