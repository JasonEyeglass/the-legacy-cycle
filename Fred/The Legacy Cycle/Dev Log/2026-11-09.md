# 2026-11-09

Back to [[The Legacy Cycle]]. The previous day's work is in [[2026-11-06]].

## Built

- **New back and side dashes:** the user made new Dash Back, Dash Left and Dash Right animations. `ReplicatedStorage.Combat.Config.Animations` now points at them:
  - DashBack = 105324889379749
  - DashLeft = 86448269982310
  - DashRight = 79958647551038
  - The forward Dash is unchanged (72481877099260).
  - Each is 0.33 s long, close to the forward Dash's 0.37 s, so the dodge's 0.6× playback stretch still fits.
  - Their `Sequence` names still point at the older AnimAuthoring KeyframeSequences, which only play in Studio when an Id is empty.

- **Shift lock on Ctrl** (`CombatClient.ShiftLock`). Roblox's own shift lock stays off (`EnableMouseLockOption = false`), because Shift sprints.
  - Ctrl (left or right) toggles it. While it's on, the mouse is locked to the centre and the cursor is hidden. The hero turns with the camera through Roblox's `CameraRelative` rotation, so lock-on and traversal moves that turn AutoRotate off still take over.
  - It steps aside while the floating menu or a full-screen screen (main menu, creator) needs the mouse, then comes back.
  - Slide lost Ctrl and keeps C and R1.
- **Shift lock indicator:** a 4 px cream dot inside a 16 px gilt ring at the screen centre. It pops in with a small overshoot and swells as it fades out.
- **Controls strip:** it now fades out fully 12 s after the HUD comes on screen (the timer used to start behind the main menu), and Caps Lock shows or hides it. Once the player uses Caps Lock, it stays the way they left it. New keycaps: Ctrl Shift Lock and Caps Hide Guides.

- **Enemy and dummies in the dev space:** `Combat.EnemyAI` now spawns a Hollow Squire at every part tagged `EnemySpawn` (the sandbox's spawn, plus a new one in `workspace.Dev` at (-130, 2990), clear of the player spawn's leash range). It also stands three **Training Dummies** in `workspace.Dev.Dummies`, west of the spawn. See [[Systems Map]].
  - A dummy has 1000 HP and is anchored, so hits don't shove it. It is stood on the ground by its hip height and heals to full 3 s after the last hit. If killed, it respawns after 5 s.
  - `Dummy = true` makes Leveling and QuestService skip it.
- **Ember cinematic:** absorbing the Ember now plays as a cutscene: shot 1 lights the embers, shot 2 collects its power, and then the floating menu opens on the Embers page. See [[Shrine VFX]].
- **Embers skill tree:** the Embers page shows a sealed tree in the rune ring once the Ember is absorbed. The reveal after the cinematic plays the 5 beats. See [[Floating Menu]].

- **Harder hit shake** (user): landing a hit now shakes the camera harder. `Feedback.shake` takes an angle boost, and only landed hits pass `Config.Feel.HitShakeBoost = 1.6`. LandLight went from 0.6 to 0.8. A light hit now swings about 2.3° (was 0.8°) and a heavy about 3.5° (was 2.2°). Taking hits, parries, guard breaks and landings shake as before.
- **Dev Ember shrine** (user): `workspace.Dev.DevShrineOfEmbers` at (-235, 3075), a copy of the Havenbrook shrine facing the spawn, out of the Squire's leash range. Its `DevReplay = true` attribute makes it replay the absorb cinematic as often as you like without touching the save:
  - EmberService fires `Absorbed` with an 8 s cooldown per player, and the prompt reads "Shrine of Embers (dev replay)".
  - ShrineFx keeps it lit and its prompt shown even with `HasEmber`, and relights it 2.5 s after it goes out.
- **Cooler cinematic** (user): rebuilt as 5 beats with a new `ShrineFx.Cinematic` module: orbit and dolly shots, a warm grade with depth of field and bloom, a flash and FOV punch on each hit, a ring of fire, a pillar of light, spiralling motes, the hero lifted while collecting, and an aura on impact. See [[Shrine VFX]].
- **Head-look fix:** Locomotion turned the hero's head toward where the camera looked, so the cutscene camera showed the back of the head. The local hero now looks straight ahead while the camera is Scriptable.
- **Clickable skill tree** (user said the nodes couldn't be clicked): each node is now a button with hover and press, and picking one shows "A Sealed Skill" with its path and step. The Ember core is a button that goes back. See [[Floating Menu]].
- **Field guide picks 1, 3 and 4** (user): from [[Roblox VFX Techniques]].
  - **Impact:** `Cinematic.impact` pops a star (two 0.05 s particles, one growing, one shrinking) on the same frame as the flash and punch, at the ignition and at the chest strike. The hero's ember aura now beats twice before it sinks in.
  - **Shockwave** (`Locomotion.ring`, used by double jumps, wall kicks and ground-pounds): the bright ring grows at a steady rate and holds before fading. A wider dark under-ring sits beneath it, and 8 chips are thrown out past it, slowing hard and shrinking. Low specs keeps only the bright ring. The guide's counter-rotating ground winds are skipped until there is a wind texture.
  - **Curves:** shrine sparks and hit sparks hold their opacity then fade, and spin at random. Shrine sparks grow fast then die away, and the cinematic motes grow in.
- **Townsfolk** (user): 12 people walk Havenbrook between 22 stops and turn their heads toward players. They say short chat-bubble remarks as you pass, and talk through reply choices answered in character by Roblox's TextGenerator, with written lines as the fallback. They follow Roblox's AI disclosure and "not extended" rules. See [[Townsfolk]].
- **Guard shield art** (user said the shield icon was missing): the shield that floats over a blocking fighter was a blue rounded box with a diamond. `CombatClient.Shield` now draws GrimoireArt's inked `icon_guard` from three copies: a gold one behind as the rim, a dim one for the spent guard, and a coloured one clipped from the bottom to the guard left (steel when full, ember when low). The rim still turns white on a blocked hit, red on a break, and dark while locked out.
- **Shrine prompts** (user): the Shrine of Embers now only offers "Absorb the Ember". StatService's respec prompt and AppearanceService's "Change appearance" prompt are gone, along with the respec code, AppearanceClient's open listener and ShrineFx's prompt puff. See [[Decisions]].
- **Townsfolk in the dev space** (user): `ServerScriptService.Townsfolk` now spawns one crowd per area. Havenbrook's people walk their own stops as before. When `workspace.Dev.Townsfolk.Stops` exists (9 stops north of the spawn), a second copy of all 12 wanders between any of them. See [[Townsfolk]].
- **Smoother shift lock** (user, "smooth and fluid"):
  - Turning on: the hero no longer snaps to the camera. They ease round (sharpness 12, up to 0.45 s), then Roblox's `CameraRelative` takes over.
  - While on: the mouse moves an aim, and the view glides after it (`Config.Camera.ShiftLockSmooth = 24`). The shoulder frames at X 1.7, Y 0.4 (`Config.Camera.ShiftLock`). Lock-on and first person skip the glide.
  - Facing fix: the shoulder offset moved the camera but not its `Focus`. Roblox turns and steers the hero along Focus − camera, so in shift lock the hero faced about 9° off the crosshair and walked that way. `CombatCamera` now moves the Focus with the offset. This also fixes the slight skew in walk direction outside shift lock.
- **Townsfolk react to bumps and hits** (user said they didn't respond):
  - Walking into someone (closing at over 1.5 studs/s, within 3.4 studs) gives them a small shove. They stop, turn to you and say a bump line.
  - Hitting one with a swing (`CombatCore.onBystanderHit`) shoves them harder and gets a "struck" line.
  - Each person has two of each line in `Townsfolk.Reactions`. Cooldowns are 4 s for bumps and 1.2 s for hits. A person talking with you doesn't react to your bumps.
  - Lines go to clients through the `TownsfolkReact` remote and show to players within 40 studs.
- **Custom speech bubble** (user, "cool and minimalist, no background"): `TownsfolkBarks.Bubble` replaces Roblox's chat bubble for remarks and reactions. It is italic Merriweather in bone with a soft shadow stroke, over a thin gold rule that fades at both ends. In 5 beats: the rule draws out, the line rises with a small overshoot, the text types in, holds for its length, then drifts up and fades. One line per head; a new line replaces the old.

- **Townsfolk AI off** (user asked to remove the "AI-powered conversation" note): Roblox requires that notice while AI answers are on, so `ServerScriptService.Townsfolk` now has `UseAI = false`, and it also defaults to false. Answers come from the written lines, and DialogueClient hides the notice because the server reports `ai = false`. See [[Decisions]].
- **Townsfolk bug fixes** (user):
  - **Dust that never stopped:** `Locomotion` only updates a body's feet-dust rate while the body is within 120 studs of the camera, so a townsperson who walked out of range kept puffing dust at their walking rate forever. Leaving range now sets the rate to 0.
  - **Endless wave:** Roblox's stock wave animation loops, and nothing stopped it. The greeting wave is now set not to loop, and ending a conversation stops it if it is still playing.
- **Ground impact only from great heights** (user): the cracked star and shockwave fired on every jump, because the "hard landing" threshold (45 studs/s) is below a normal jump's landing speed (about 53 with JumpHeight 7.2). `Traversal.land` now draws them only at `Config.Traversal.ImpactLanding = 110` (about a 30-stud drop), sized by how far past that the fall was. The lighter shake and thud above 45 are unchanged.
- **Trove added** (user): `ReplicatedStorage.Packages.Trove`, Sleitnick's Trove v1.8.0 (MIT), fetched from the official RbxUtil repository and read in full first. It is one file that only uses RunService. A smoke test destroyed a tracked folder, ran a cleanup function, disconnected a connection and cleaned a nested trove. Nothing uses it yet.
- **Wind shockwave and double-jump VFX** (user, "shouldn't have orange, it should be like wind"): a new `CombatClient.Wind` module.
  - `Wind.ring` replaces `Locomotion.ring`, which is deleted, for double jumps, wall kicks and ground-pounds. It flings out 8 pale gust arcs (4 on low specs) that swirl about 60° as they spread to about 6 studs, every other one on a tighter, later circle, over a flat ring of soft air puffs.
  - `Wind.burst` replaces the double-jump and wall-kick burst (an orange flash, speed lines, afterimages and ember sparks): 3 gusts (2 on low specs) spiral up round the body from the feet, tightening as they rise, and are left behind as the hero climbs.
  - A gust is a Beam bent along a circular arc (curve handles of 4/3·tan(θ/4)·r), with a thin, shaded tail trailing to a white head. It snaps in, holds, thins and fades out.
  - **Trove in use:** each effect owns a Trove holding its attachments, beams, puff emitter and frame loop, cleaned in one call when it ends.
  - `Wind.dash` replaces the dodge's white-hot flash, orange glow and orange-and-white speed lines (user said yes). Gusts wrap round the dash line at the launch point, leaning the way you went, and slide back and widen as you shoot away (5 gusts, 3 on low specs, 0.32 s). It covers ground dodges, air dashes, the running heavy's lunge and other players' dodges. The afterimages and dust kick stay.
  - Checked in Edit: the module loads, its self-checks pass (the arc midpoint stays on the circle, and the fade curve starts and ends hidden), and nothing calls `Locomotion.ring` any more. Not seen in play.
- **Wall climb rework** (user, "like climbing a cliff or a ladder"): the old climb ran straight up the wall for a fixed time. Now you grab the wall and stay on it.
  - Moving toward the wall climbs (9 studs/s), away climbs down (12), and across goes sideways (8). With no input you hang still. A LinearVelocity holds the body, so gravity doesn't drag you off.
  - Sideways stops where the wall ends, and up stops under an overhang. A ledge still hands over to the mantle, a jump still kicks off, and touching the ground lets go.
  - **Stamina:** the server drains 12 a second while you climb and move faster than 2 studs/s (`CombatCore.climb`, fed by the new `ClimbStart` and `ClimbEnd` actions). Hanging still costs nothing, but stamina doesn't recover on the wall. At 0 you can only climb down.
  - The climb animation's speed follows your movement: it plays backwards going down and freezes while you hang.
  - Config: `ClimbSpeed`, `ClimbDownSpeed`, `ClimbSideSpeed`, `ClimbStamina` and `ClimbGrab` replace `WallClimbTime` and `WallClimbSpeed`.
- **Rock ground impact** (user, "trove is like ground impact that spawns rocks"): a new `CombatClient.Rocks` replaces the dark cracked star (`Locomotion.impact`, now deleted). Landing from a great height bursts a rim of tilted slabs up from the ground, in the ground's own material and colour (terrain or part). Chunks fly out and come to rest, and dust kicks up. It all sinks back after about 1.6 s. Low specs get 6 slabs and 4 chunks. Trove (the cleanup library) cleans it up.
- Checked in Edit: Rocks, Wind, Traversal, Locomotion, CombatCore and CombatService all compile, and Rocks and Wind load. Every climb setting Traversal reads exists in Config, and nothing still refers to the star or the old climb settings. Not seen in play.
- **Main menu text centred, bigger and bolder** (user):
  - The seal-button labels were centred on the whole piece (seal and curl included), so they sat about 12 px right of the parchment. Measured from the atlas, the parchment spans 30–90% of `mbtn` and 11–90% of `sbtn`. `MainMenu.PARCHMENT` holds those spans, and labels centre on them.
  - The subtitle "Prologue · The Last Ember" was left-aligned; it is now centred under the title and 17 px.
  - Labels use TextScaled up to a cap (Play 32, Settings 24, Character Customization 18, Back 22). Fondamento has no bold weight, so a thin ink UIStroke thickens it.
