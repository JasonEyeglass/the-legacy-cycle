# 2026-11-09

Back to [[The Legacy Cycle]]. The previous day's work is in [[2026-11-06]].

## Built

- **New back and side dashes:** the user made new Dash Back, Dash Left and Dash Right animations. `ReplicatedStorage.Combat.Config.Animations` now points at them:
  - DashBack = 105324889379749
  - DashLeft = 86448269982310
  - DashRight = 79958647551038
  - The forward Dash is unchanged (72481877099260).
  - Each is 0.33 s long, close to the forward Dash's 0.37 s, so the dodge's 0.6× playback stretch still fits.
  - Their `Sequence` names still point at the older AnimAuthoring KeyframeSequences, which only play in Studio when an Id is empty.

- **Shift lock on Ctrl** (`CombatClient.ShiftLock`). Roblox's own shift lock stays off (`EnableMouseLockOption = false`), because Shift sprints.
  - Ctrl (left or right) toggles it. While it's on, the mouse is locked to the centre and the cursor is hidden. The hero turns with the camera through Roblox's `CameraRelative` rotation, so lock-on and traversal moves that turn AutoRotate off still take over.
  - It steps aside while the floating menu or a full-screen screen (main menu, creator) needs the mouse, then comes back.
  - Slide lost Ctrl and keeps C and R1.
- **Shift lock indicator:** a 4 px cream dot inside a 16 px gilt ring at the screen centre. It pops in with a small overshoot and swells as it fades out.
- **Controls strip:** it now fades out fully 12 s after the HUD comes on screen (the timer used to start behind the main menu), and Caps Lock shows or hides it. Once the player uses Caps Lock, it stays the way they left it. New keycaps: Ctrl Shift Lock and Caps Hide Guides.

## Verified in playtests

- All three uploads load on the character.
- Real dodges (Q with the katana drawn) played DashBack standing still, DashRight strafing right and DashLeft strafing left. The console was clean.
- Shift lock: Ctrl locked the mouse and turned the hero to face the camera exactly. Opening the menu (M) freed the mouse and hid the mark, and closing it brought both back. The strip showed after Play, faded at 12 s, and Caps Lock hid and showed it. The console was clean.
- **Not tested:** mouse-look while locked (Studio's input tool can't move the mouse), and shift lock during dialogue choices.
